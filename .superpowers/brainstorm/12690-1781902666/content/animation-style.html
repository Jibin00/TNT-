Which animation style feels right?

Three directions for motion + interaction polish across the whole page. Pick the one that matches the vibe you want.

A · Subtle & Elegant

Motion is felt, not seen.

  • Sections fade in softly as you scroll
  • Cards lift 2px on hover with a slight shadow
  • Buttons: gentle background + border color shift (already in)
  • Form fields: smooth focus ring expansion
  • Timing: 200–300ms, ease-out curves
  • Reduced motion: all motion disabled

B · Expressive & Polished

Motion rewards attention.

  • Cards stagger-reveal on scroll (60ms delay between each)
  • Card hover: scale 1.02 + cyan glow + elevated shadow
  • Buttons: subtle press feedback (scale 0.98 on click)
  • Hero headline: character or word-by-word reveal on load
  • Form: success card slides in + checkmark animation
  • Timing: 300–500ms, custom cubic-bezier curves
  • Reduced motion: crossfade only, no scale/translate

C · Minimal & Fast

Hover only. No scroll effects.

  • No entrance or scroll-triggered animations
  • Cards get a slightly different border color on hover
  • Buttons: same hover as today, snappier (100ms)
  • Focus rings: instantaneous, no transition
  • Timing: 100–150ms, linear
  • Reduced motion: identical (no motion to disable)
