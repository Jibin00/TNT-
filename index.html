Frequently asked questions

  • You can make a conscious call based on the inspection report. A detailed health report helps you make the right decision for your used car purchase.

  • A second-hand car should be inspected through a structured pre-delivery inspection (PDI) covering exterior, interior, engine, electrical systems, legal status, and market value. A professional used car inspection service ensures every critical component is thoroughly assessed before purchase.

  • Pre-delivery inspections (PDI) are not mandatory for used cars, but they are an important service that can detect hidden flaws and intentional cover-ups in a second-hand car and bring it to the right value.

  • If a car fails the PDI, the issues identified will be documented in a detailed report. This allows the buyer to renegotiate the deal, request repairs, or choose not to proceed with the purchase, ensuring no unwanted surprises post-sale.

  • The used car PDI checklist, which includes 140+ point checks, includes exterior damage, tyre wear, engine health, fluid levels, brake performance, interior features, battery condition, ECU errors, RC verification, insurance history, challans, and a real-time market valuation.
