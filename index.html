How it works

Inspect. Verify. Buy. Deliver.

Four stages, one point of contact, and no step where you are asked to simply take our word for it.

  1. 01

    Inspect

    We reach the vehicle in person and run a structured mechanical, cosmetic and structural inspection — photographed and filmed throughout.

  2. 02

    Verify

    Ownership, service history, odometer authenticity, insurance and the seller themselves are checked against the documents.

  3. 03

    Buy

    You get a plain-language report and a recommendation. If it's worth buying, we use the findings to negotiate on your behalf.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Secure, insured transport anywhere in India, with a final pre-delivery check and a proper handover at your door.
